# The Tomorrow Company — Project Brief

**Client:** The Tomorrow Company · USA · Technology Venture Studio
**Site:** tomorrowcompany.com
**Contact on the client side:** Stephen
**Staging:** https://tomorrow.esvgco.workers.dev (Cloudflare Workers, auto-deploy from GitHub `esvgco/tomorrow`)

## Company

A new technology venture studio that combines expertise in technology, entrepreneurship and
the entertainment industry. It builds its own products, companies and intellectual property
at the intersection of AI, physical computing and new user scenarios.

One of the projects in development is a recruiting platform for technology companies which,
according to Stephen, already has its first clients.

## Task

Refine the existing website concept and launch a complete responsive website: design,
animation and development.

**Core idea:** a rabbit that explores the hole, disappears and reappears in another part of the
site, gradually revealing information about the company and its projects.

## Visual direction

- Extremely minimal site, 90–95% white space.
- Black typography, minimal graphics, intelligent interactive elements.
- The technology shows in how the site *behaves*, not in typical AI visuals.
- Full client wording: [website-direction.md](website-direction.md).

## Studio notes (from the kickoff with the team lead)

- The client imagines the rabbit drawn as a contour line and the hole as solid black.
  We don't have to follow that strictly — we want to explore other kinds of rabbits too.
- End goal: an **animated rabbit character with a rig** that can run and jump naturalistically
  and that we can **control on the site**.
- Order of work:
  1. Decide what the rabbit looks like → that decides 2D vs 3D → that decides how it is rigged.
  2. Build the rig right away.
  3. Fine-tune the look.
  4. Only then start on the web page itself.
