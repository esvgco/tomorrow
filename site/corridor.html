The Tomorrow Company builds at the edge of what is becoming possible.

Co-founded by Cyan Banister and Stephen Hamel, the company explores how technology can become more personal, intuitive and human.

We bring together ideas, technology and expertise to pursue opportunities that conventional approaches might overlook.

From emerging forms of AI and physical computing to new models for building companies, solving complex problems and creating original stories and worlds, we work with people and organizations who recognize what's becoming possible to transform ideas into meaningful products, businesses and experiences.

Cyan Banister is an entrepreneur and technology investor known for her independent thinking, unconventional instincts, and ability to recognize extraordinary potential long before it becomes obvious. She is drawn to ideas that challenge conventional thinking and founders who see opportunities where others see obstacles. Her early investments include SpaceX, Uber, DeepMind, Niantic, Postmates and Affirm.

Stephen Hamel has spent much of his career developing and producing film, television and original intellectual property, including a longstanding creative partnership with Keanu Reeves. His work has centered on finding ambitious ideas, shaping them from their earliest stages, and bringing together the creative talent, partners and resources required to make them real.
