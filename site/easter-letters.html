01 — A beginning

You saw a letter.
There was a whole room.

Inside the “o” in tomorrow.

The wall, from afar / Study 04.6

A few ways in.

  • Scroll to read. At the small door, keep scrolling to step back.
  • Drag empty space, or use ← →, to turn. You can turn from the beginning.
  • At the foot of the wall, move the pointer toward an edge to look around. Use A / D to walk sideways, W / S to move forward and back. Walk past the edge to get behind the wall.
  • Click an o in the opening or final heading to enter the room.
  • Undo an unexpected edit with ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z.
  • Click the space between words. The browser’s Back button returns you to the previous day.
  • On a phone: swipe to move; swipe sideways to turn. Tap an o in the final heading to enter the room.
