Rabbit study 01 · one rig, four rabbits

Four rabbits, one skeleton.

One motion rig, four interpretations. Soft geometric has its own rounded proportions and curved ears. The top row alternates between an alert sit and a curious look into the hole. Drag to orbit, or hold either pose. The bottom row runs the gallop cycle keyed from the reference footage.

AModel · sitting by the holedrag to orbit
01Contour

Ink line on white, as the client's mark. Silhouette and occlusion contours come from the 3D body, so the line stays right from any angle.

02Pixel

1-bit, about 20 px tall. White pixel seams where one part passes in front of another — the eye, ear, haunch and legs.

03Soft geometric

A round head, short muzzle, pill-shaped paws and broad curved ears. Sky: white volumes dissolving into blue, blue eye, red nose. Gradient: orange-red → pink → white, from the nose back or by how each surface faces the camera.

04PS1 low-poly

Rigid segments, few flat faces, 32 px textures, snapped vertices, affine texture warp, 15-bit dither — as in Unity 8.
