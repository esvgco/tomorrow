Rabbit study 01 · one rig, four rabbits

Four rabbits, one skeleton.

Every rabbit below is the same rig — 28 bones, 21 primitives — drawn four different ways. The top row sits by the hole — looking around, then settling down to sniff it — drag to turn it around, switch on the rig to see the bones. The bottom row runs the half-bound: hind feet plant together far forward under a rounded back, the back opens into a line as they push, the forelegs shoot forward only in flight, then catch the landing while the hind legs fold up like a spring and roll forward along the belly.

AModel · by the hole, looking around and sniffingdrag to orbit
01Contour

Ink line on white, as the client's mark. Silhouette and occlusion contours come from the 3D body, so the line stays right from any angle.

02Pixel

1-bit, about 20 px tall. White pixel seams where one part passes in front of another — the eye, ear, haunch and legs.

03Soft geometric

Its own, cruder set of primitives on the same skeleton — two sausage ears, a sausage face, a ball of a rump — melted together like metaballs. Sky: white volumes dissolving into blue, blue eye, red nose. Gradient: orange-red → pink → white, from the nose back or by how each surface faces the camera.

04PS1 low-poly

Rigid segments, few flat faces, 32 px textures, snapped vertices, affine texture warp, 15-bit dither — as in Unity 8.
