The Tomorrow Company — working studies

  1. 04.3The tube

    The same screens wound round the inside of a shaft. Scrolling takes you down and round: each screen waits a little lower and a little further to the side, and the sides of the view bend towards you like a curved screen. The rabbit is alone on the first screen. Past the last one, look down and fall into tomorrow.

  2. 04.4The tube, no guides

    The same tube without the drawn grooves and ribs: only the screens, the bend and the hole at the bottom. For comparison.

  3. 04.2The wall 2

    One continuous sheet, direct scrolling and a slight perspective tilt.

  4. 05.2A little ahead 2

    One reading column. Less composition, the same hidden passages.

  5. 04The wall

    A white wall, a changing scale, and a small door. Reading becomes moving through space. Turn around, walk beyond the page, or find a room inside a letter. A full homepage with the text, time and photographic experiments together.

  6. 05A little ahead

    An editorial homepage with something happening in the margins. A full stop is an entrance, photographs take their time, and tomorrow arrives a little early.

  7. 01Four rabbits, one skeleton

    The rig and its four looks. Sit, gallop, hop.

  8. 01Homepage concept 01

    The field, what we do, projects as holes, say hello. Also arrival and the playground.

  9. 06The portal

    Their first screen. Scroll down: their site, one column. Scroll up: the head goes into the hole, the hole opens into a circle, the circle fills the screen and runs black → blue → orange → lilac → white, and the same content is there again, scrolled forward into depth. Scroll back past the start, or Esc, to return.

  10. 02The corridor

    The rabbit goes in, the hole flattens into a line and the first sentence surfaces through it. Every rule is the same hole edge-on. You walk away from the hole and get smaller without noticing. Scroll past the end to turn around: it was behind you. Go in and it is tomorrow.

  11. 03Someone else is here

    A paragraph falls down its own columns and is a list on the way to being the next paragraph. A line is highlighted as it leaves the screen; scroll back and nothing happened. Then someone edits a sentence in front of you. Cmd+Z. Click the space between two words to fly through the letters into tomorrow; Back returns.
