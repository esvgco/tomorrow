Some things reward
a second look.

  • The full stop in “ahead” is an entrance. Stay on it, or click.
  • Pause over a photograph. On a phone, tap it.
  • Click a space between words in any text to fly through. Select a word or paragraph to look closer.
  • Someone might edit a sentence. ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z gives you the last word.
  • The hole at the bottom takes you to tomorrow. Back returns you to today.
