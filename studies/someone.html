The Tomorrow Company's projects and original IP shouldn't look like they all belong to the same product category. The company itself needs to be the organizing idea. It probably shouldn't look technical. It should behave technically: the technology comes through in how the site moves, responds and reveals things.

Projects don't need to appear in a grid. They can be discovered as you move. The experience should be extremely simple, but gradually reveal more as you explore it. Simple to navigate. Interesting to explore.
